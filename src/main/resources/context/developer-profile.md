## Company overview

Grupo Vértice is a real estate developer focused on premium communities along Mexico's Pacific coast. With over two decades of experience, the company creates high-end, master-planned communities, residential spaces, and commercial developments that elevate quality of life and deliver durable capital appreciation.

## Key pillars

Vision: Turning landscapes into sustainable, high-value master-planned communities.
Innovation: Modern architecture, state-of-the-art amenities, and natural surroundings.
Trust and solidity: Structured financial models, institutional investments, and a proven record of timely delivery.

## Strategic value proposition

Master-planned communities: "Cities within cities" with controlled access and 24/7 security, integrated commercial areas, schools, and recreational spaces, plus underground utilities, premium paving, and eco-friendly waste management.
Sustainability and environment: Respect for Pacific ecosystems, mangrove reserves, and Sierra Madre topography, with extensive green areas, natural lakes, preserved flora and fauna, energy-efficient technology, and sustainable water management.
Financial return and plusvalía: Locations in high-growth corridors such as Guadalajara, Puerto Vallarta, and Baja California Sur, with historically solid capital appreciation for institutional and private investors.

## Notable projects

Punta Esmeralda Golf & Country Club: flagship master-planned community with the championship El Mirador golf course.
Mirador Residencial: mixed-use urban development in Guadalajara with residential towers, corporate offices, and a commercial lifestyle center.
Torre Altavista: luxury vertical residential towers in Puerto Vallarta.
The Reserve at Punta Esmeralda: high-end residential offerings within the Punta Esmeralda ecosystem.

## Corporate culture and leadership

Commitment to excellence: Collaboration with renowned architects, urban planners, and designers.
Institutional governance: Strict compliance, transparency, and institutional standards suitable for international investment and real estate funds.
Community impact: Direct and indirect jobs that support economic development in the Pacific region of Mexico.
